We are delighted to announce that our paper **"Automatic Design of Optimization
Test Problems with Large Language Models"** has been accepted for publication in
**IEEE Transactions on Evolutionary Computation**!

Standard benchmark suites such as BBOB offer limited coverage of possible
optimization landscapes. We use LLM-guided evolution to generate benchmark
functions with specified landscape characteristics: an LLM proposes and modifies
readable Python code, while evolutionary selection favors functions whose
measured landscape features best match a target profile.

The paper is available on [IEEE Xplore](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/11719708/),
and the code on [GitHub](https://github.com/helix-agh/ela-guided-llm-bench).
