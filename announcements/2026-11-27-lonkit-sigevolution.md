Our work made it into the November 2026 issue of **SIGEVOlution**, the
newsletter of ACM SIGEVO (Volume 19, Issue 4)!

The [cover of the issue](https://evolution.sigevo.org/issues/HTML/sigevolution-19-4/index.html#About_the_Cover)
was created with **lonkit**. It shows how the fitness landscape of Number
Partitioning changes across three instances of increasing difficulty: a single
global funnel breaks up into many competing funnels.

![Local Optima Networks of three Number Partitioning instances of increasing difficulty, made with lonkit for the SIGEVOlution cover](assets/news/2026-11-sigevolution-cover-800.webp)

The issue also features a short [announcement of lonkit](https://evolution.sigevo.org/issues/HTML/sigevolution-19-4/index.html#lonkit_Local_Optima_Networks_in_Python),
our open-source Python library for building, analyzing and visualizing Local
Optima Networks.
