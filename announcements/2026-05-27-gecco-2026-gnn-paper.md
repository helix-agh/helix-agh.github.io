We are delighted to announce that our paper **"[Analyzing search behavior of
population-based optimization algorithms using graph neural networks](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3795095.3805195)"** has been
accepted at [GECCO 2026](https://gecco-2026.sigevo.org/).

The work studies how graph neural networks can characterize and explain the
search behavior of population-based optimization algorithms.
