Our preprint **"Automatic Design of Optimization Test Problems with Large
Language Models"** is now available on
[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.02724).

The paper introduces **Evolution of Test Functions (EoTF)**, a framework that
automatically generates continuous optimization test functions whose landscapes
match a specified target Exploratory Landscape Analysis (ELA) feature vector.
EoTF adapts LLM-driven evolutionary search to evolve interpretable, self-contained
numpy implementations of objective functions, offering a scalable and portable
route to benchmark generation targeted to desired landscape properties.
