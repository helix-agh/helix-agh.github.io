On 18–19 November 2026 we hosted our partners from the **Faculty of Applied
Informatics, Tomas Bata University in Zlín** (Zuzana Komínková Oplatková,
Libor Pekař and Petr Chalupa) for a two-day joint workshop at AGH.

![HELIX and UTB Zlín researchers at the joint workshop at AGH, November 2026](assets/news/2026-11-utb-zlin-workshop-800.webp)

The goal was to agree on the problems we will work on within our
partnership. We discussed optimization problems from control engineering that
we plan to tackle together.

We are looking forward to the next steps of the collaboration!
