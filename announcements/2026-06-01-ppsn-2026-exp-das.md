We are extremely happy and proud to announce, that a paper we prepared: 
**"[RL-Exponential-DAS: Exponential Decision Schedules for Dynamic Algorithm Selection](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-032-36217-9_10)"** has been accepted at [PPSN 2026](https://ppsn2026.disi.unitn.it/).

The work studies how the scheduling of
optimization-evaluation-decision periods
impacts the performance and behaviour of population-based optimization
algorithms.
